01

Overview

A single-page website for an India-based firm offering white-label bookkeeping and an "India desk" for NRI and cross-border tax to CPA and accounting firms abroad.

Audience

CPA and accounting firms in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE looking for offshore capacity.

Goal

Get visitors to book a free 30-minute call or send an enquiry through the contact form.

Tech

One static index.html file. No database or server. Hosted free on Netlify, with the form handled by Netlify Forms.

05

Host it on Netlify

Free hosting with HTTPS, your own domain and a working contact form. Takes about 30 minutes, plus up to a day for the domain change to take effect.

  1. Prepare the file

    • Download index.html from section 3.
    • Open it in a text editor (Notepad or VS Code) and replace every [bracket] with your details.
    • Create a folder named aceecofin and put index.html inside. Later you can add a photo (e.g. founder.jpg) to the same folder.

  2. Create a Netlify account

    • Go to netlify.com → Sign up (email or Google). The free plan is enough.

  3. Deploy the site

    • Go to Add new project → Deploy manually.
    • Drag the aceecofin folder onto the upload area.
    • After a few seconds the site is live at a temporary address like random-name.netlify.app. Open it and check everything.
    • Optional: Project configuration → Change project name to aceecofin.

  4. Add your domain

    • In the site: Domain management → Add a domain → type aceecofinadvisors.com → Verify → Add domain.
    • Netlify will also set up www.aceecofinadvisors.com.

  5. Point the domain to Netlify

    Log in to the registrar where you bought the domain (GoDaddy, Hostinger, Namecheap, etc.) and choose one option:

    Option A (easiest): use Netlify DNS. In Netlify choose Set up Netlify DNS. It shows four nameservers. At your registrar, replace the existing nameservers with those four.

    dns1.p0X.nsone.net
dns2.p0X.nsone.net
dns3.p0X.nsone.net
dns4.p0X.nsone.net   ← use the exact names Netlify shows you

    Option B: keep your registrar's DNS. Add these records at the registrar (Netlify shows the exact values under Awaiting External DNS):

    TypeName / HostValue
    A@The IP address Netlify shows (load balancer)
    CNAMEwwwyour-site-name.netlify.app
    Delete any old A or CNAME records for @ and www (e.g. the registrar's "parking" page), or they will conflict.

  6. Wait, then turn on HTTPS

    • DNS changes usually take 1–2 hours, sometimes up to 24.
    • Netlify issues a free SSL certificate by itself. If it doesn't, go to Domain management → HTTPS → Verify DNS configuration.
    • Test both https://aceecofinadvisors.com and the www version.

  7. Switch on contact form emails

    • The form is already set up for Netlify Forms (data-netlify="true", name contact, with spam protection).
    • After deploying, open Forms in Netlify, enable form detection if asked, and redeploy once.
    • Under Forms → Form notifications → Add notification → Email notification, enter your business email.
    • Send a test enquiry from the live site to check it arrives.
    • The free plan includes 100 submissions a month.

  8. Update the site later

    • Edit index.html on your computer.
    • In Netlify go to Deploys and drag the folder in again. The site updates in seconds, and you can roll back to any earlier version with one click.
06

Business email with your domain

Use name@aceecofinadvisors.com instead of Gmail. CPAs take it more seriously, and it's free.

  1. Sign up for Zoho Mail's free plan

    zoho.com/mail → Pricing → Forever Free (up to 5 users, 5 GB each, web and mobile apps). Enter aceecofinadvisors.com as your domain.

  2. Verify the domain

    Zoho gives you a TXT record. Add it in Netlify DNS (if you chose Option A) or at your registrar (Option B).

  3. Add the mail records

    TypeHostValue (from Zoho)
    MX@mx.zoho.in (priority 10), mx2.zoho.in (20), mx3.zoho.in (50). Use exactly what Zoho shows
    TXT (SPF)@v=spf1 include:zoho.in ~all
    TXT (DKIM)the selector Zoho givesThe long key from Zoho → Domains → DKIM

  4. Create your mailbox

    For example mayank@ and contact@aceecofinadvisors.com. Use it on the website, the contact form notifications, LinkedIn and your firm profile.
